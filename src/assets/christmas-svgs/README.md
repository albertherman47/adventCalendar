# Your Christmas SVG artwork

Drop your `.svg` files into this folder. The Printable Resources page automatically picks SVGs from here and randomly assigns them to printable worksheet headers and greeting cards. Each spot keeps its selected SVG while the page rerenders; a new random choice is made on a fresh page load.

Use self-contained SVGs with a `viewBox` so they scale cleanly. Landscape artwork works best for worksheet headers; compact or square artwork works best on the greeting cards. Files are displayed as images and are not inlined into the page.
